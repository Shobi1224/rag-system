# KIP-915: TxnOffsetCommit with auto-committed offsets

## KIP-915: TxnOffsetCommit with Auto-Committed Offsets

### Summary
Resolved race conditions between background auto-committed consumer offsets and active transactional offset commits in `KafkaConsumer`.
